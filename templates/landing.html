Blinded assessment, built for rigorous research.

UDMM Research Workspace brings patient records, coded radiographs, and age estimates into one careful workflow, so every assessment is made without knowing whose image it is.

Enter workspace See how it works

How it works

Four steps, one clear path from record to result.

  1. 01

    Record

    Add patients one at a time or import a spreadsheet, then attach each panoramic radiograph.

  2. 02

    Blind

    Every case receives separate codes for each method, so identities never reach the assessor.

  3. 03

    Assess

    Principal investigators work through a queue of coded images and record an estimate for each.

  4. 04

    Analyse

    Supervisors compare estimates with chronological age and export the full dataset for study.

Principles

Designed around the integrity of the study.

Blinded by design

Assessors see a code, an image, and a sex. Names and ages stay with the supervisor.

Two methods, side by side

AlQahtani and Demirjian are assessed independently under different codes for a fair comparison.

Ready for analysis

Differences are calculated as you go, and the full record exports to Excel with images attached.

Our approach

An estimate is only as trustworthy as the process behind it. We keep the assessor focused on the image, and nothing else.
